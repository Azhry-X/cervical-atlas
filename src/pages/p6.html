1 · Cervical Collar & Mechanical Traction KUIJPER · GRAHAM · FRITZ · ROMEO
Collar or physio vs wait-and-see (recent radiculopathy) (Kuijper 2009 BMJ) H: collar + rest or 6 weeks physio + home exercise: extra arm-pain reduction 12 mm vs wait-and-see at 6 weeks; neck pain −17 mm (collar), −14 mm (physio).
Traction (Cochrane) (Graham 2008 Cochrane) M: no low-risk-of-bias evidence that clearly supports or refutes continuous or intermittent traction.
Traction + exercise (RCT, radiculopathy) (Fritz 2014 JOSPT) M: NDI lower at 6 months with mechanical traction vs exercise alone (MD 13.3, 5.6–21.0) and vs over-door traction (8.1, 0.8–15.3); at 12 months vs exercise (9.8, 0.2–19.4).
Traction + PT (MA) (Romeo 2018 Phys Ther) M: mechanical traction: pain g −0.85 (−1.63 to −0.06) short term, −1.17 (−2.25 to −0.10) intermediate; disability g −1.05 (−1.81 to −0.28) intermediate.
2 · Low-Level Laser & Electrotherapy CHOW 2009 · KROELING 2013
Low-level laser (MA, 16 RCTs, 820 pts) (Chow 2009 Lancet) M: acute: RR 1.69 (1.22–2.33); chronic: RR 4.05 (2.74–5.98); VAS −19.86 mm (10.04–29.68); follow-up 1–22 weeks −22.07 mm (17.42–26.72).
Electrotherapy (Cochrane, 20 trials) (Kroeling 2013 Cochrane) L: very low quality; TENS inconsistent; galvanic current, iontophoresis, EMS no better than placebo.
3 · JOSPT 2017 CPG & OPTIMa (NAD Grades I–III) Passive Modality Recommendations BLANPIED 2017 · CÔTÉ 2016
JOSPT CPG — radiating pain Blanpied 2017 (Guideline): acute: “may provide mobilizing and stabilizing exercises, laser, and short-term use of a cervical collar”; chronic: “should provide mechanical intermittent cervical traction, combined with other interventions such as stretching and strengthening exercise plus cervical and thoracic mobilization/manipulation”.
JOSPT CPG — chronic mobility deficits / WAD (Blanpied 2017 JOSPT, Guideline): chronic mobility deficits: “Dry needling, laser, or intermittent mechanical/manual traction” within a multimodal approach; WAD (low risk) and chronic WAD: may provide TENS.
OPTIMa (NAD grades I–III) Côté 2016 (Guideline): grades I–II ≤ 3 months: “should not offer … cervical collar, electroacupuncture, electrotherapy, or clinic-based heat”; grades I–II > 3 months: may consider low-level laser; should not offer electrotherapy, shortwave diathermy, clinic-based heat; grade III ≤ 3 months: should not offer cervical collar, low-level laser or traction; grade III > 3 months: no collar.
