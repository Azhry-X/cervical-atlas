1 · Neurodynamic & Provocative Tests for Cervical Radiculopathy RUBINSTEIN 2007 · WAINNER 2003 · THOOMES 2018
Provocative tests (SR) (Rubinstein 2007 Eur Spine J) M: Spurling, traction/distraction and Valsalva: low–moderate Sn, high Sp → rule in; ULTT high Sn, low Sp → negative rules out; shoulder abduction test low–moderate Sn, moderate–high Sp.
Single tests — Wainner 2003 data (SR table, needle EMG/NCS reference) (Rubinstein 2007 Eur Spine J) M: ULTT A: Sn 0.97 (0.90–1.0), Sp 0.22 (0.12–0.33) · Spurling A: Sn 0.50 (0.27–0.73), Sp 0.86 (0.77–0.94) · distraction: Sn 0.44 (0.21–0.67), Sp 0.90 (0.82–0.98) · Valsalva: Sn 0.22 (0.03–0.41), Sp 0.94 (0.88–1.0) · shoulder abduction: Sn 0.17 (0.0–0.34), Sp 0.92 (0.85–0.99).
Spurling & combinations (SR) (Thoomes 2018 Spine J) M: Sp 0.89–1.00; Sn 0.38–0.97; combine Spurling + axial traction + arm squeeze to rule in; 4 negative neurodynamic tests + negative arm squeeze to rule out.
